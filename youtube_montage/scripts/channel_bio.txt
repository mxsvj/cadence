DESCRIPTION (Personnaliser la chaîne → Infos de base → Description)
Money, explained for Europeans.

Euro Unboxed takes one big money topic at a time (crypto, ETFs, pensions, savings, the digital euro) and opens it up box by box, in plain English and in a few minutes.

Made for people who live in Europe: EU rules like MiCA and UCITS, prices in euros, and the products you can actually buy here.

No hype. No price predictions. No "get rich quick". Just clear explanations, with the sources in every description.

New explainers every week.

Education only, not financial advice.
