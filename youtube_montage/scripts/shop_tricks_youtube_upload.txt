You go online to buy one thing and leave with four. That's not bad luck, it's design. Here are 7 tricks shops use: prices ending in 9, decoys, fake countdowns, fake discounts, free-shipping thresholds, buy now pay later and the store map. The research behind each one, the EU rules that protect you, and how to beat them.

Education only, not financial advice.
