DESCRIPTION (à coller telle quelle)
Put €10,000 away, never touch it, and 30 years later it's worth about €76,000 (at 7% a year). That's compound interest. Here are 7 simple ideas: interest on interest, the rule of 72, why time beats money, why most people quit during the boring years, and how debt, fees and inflation compound against you.

Education only, not financial advice.
