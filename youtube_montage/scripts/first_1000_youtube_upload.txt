DESCRIPTION (à coller telle quelle)
Everyone has a plan for your first €1,000. Most of them are wrong. Here are 8 steps, in order: kill expensive debt, keep a small cushion (and if €1,000 is all you have, that's your cushion), use the right account (PEA, ISA, ISK…), pick a broker regulated where you live, buy the whole market with one global index ETF, automate it, don't touch it, and keep learning. Investing can lose money.

Education only, not financial advice.
