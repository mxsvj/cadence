DESCRIPTION (à coller telle quelle)
Index, sector, thematic, bonds, gold, leveraged, crypto… there are about 16,000 ETFs in the world, but almost all of them fit into 12 boxes. Here's every box, explained in 11 minutes, with a focus on what's different for investors in Europe (UCITS, Acc vs Dist, ETCs, ETPs, why you can't buy VOO).

No hype, no stock picks. ETFs can lose value. Education only, not financial advice.
