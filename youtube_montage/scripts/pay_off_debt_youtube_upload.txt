DESCRIPTION (à coller telle quelle)
You pay every month and the debt barely moves. Here are 6 steps to get out faster: look at the whole picture, escape the minimum payment, pick an order (avalanche or snowball), focus on one debt at a time, make the interest smaller, and don't refill the bucket.

Education only, not financial advice.
